Learning that helps every girl thrive

A supportive approach to knowledge, practical skills, and positive values.

We aim to make learning active, encouraging, and relevant to each pupil. Through a strong foundation in primary education, girls are supported to ask questions, practise new skills, and take pride in their progress.

Core foundations

Build confidence in reading, writing, mathematics, communication, and the essential knowledge pupils use across their learning.

Learning by doing

Encourage curiosity and understanding through discussion, practice, creative work, and age-appropriate problem-solving.

Values and wellbeing

Support respect, responsibility, cooperation, and confidence as part of each girl's learning and personal development.

A pathway through primary school

Learning develops step by step, from early foundations to greater independence.

Grades 1–3

Building foundations

Pupils strengthen essential skills and develop positive routines that help them engage confidently with school and learning.

  • Practise early literacy and numeracy
  • Explore ideas through guided activities
  • Build communication and learning habits

Grades 4–6

Growing independence

Pupils extend their knowledge, make connections across subjects, and take increasing responsibility for their learning.

  • Develop deeper understanding of core concepts
  • Apply skills to tasks and practical projects
  • Strengthen confidence and self-directed learning

Learning beyond the lesson

A broad range of experiences can help pupils discover interests and practise new skills.

Reading

Build a love of reading, vocabulary, and understanding through regular practice.

Creative expression

Make space for imagination and self-expression through creative activities.

Sport and movement

Encourage teamwork, participation, and healthy development through physical activity.

Exploration

Connect learning with the world around us through observation and practical activities.

Progress grows with support

Every pupil learns in her own way. Ongoing guidance and communication help families and the school support each girl's development.

Practice and feedback

Regular learning activities help pupils practise skills and understand what to work on next.

Family communication

Connect with the school to discuss your child's learning and progress.

Confidence and effort

Recognise curiosity, persistence, and the growth that comes from trying.
