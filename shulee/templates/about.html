AIC Laboret Girls Boarding Primary School

Growing through learning and community

We are a girls' boarding primary school in Nandi North Sub-County, Kenya, committed to a supportive learning environment grounded in education, Christian values, and care for every pupil.

A place to learn, belong, and grow

AIC Laboret Girls Boarding Primary School brings pupils together for learning and school life in a boarding environment. We aim to help every girl build knowledge, confidence, responsibility, and respect for others.

Families are important partners in each pupil's education. The school welcomes questions about academics, admissions, events, and the arrangements that support pupils during the school year.

Learning with purpose

Our focus is on strong foundations, steady effort, curiosity, and the growth of each pupil as a learner and member of the school community.

What guides our school

Our community is shaped by the values we practise in learning and in our relationships with one another.

Learning

Encouraging curiosity, perseverance, and a secure foundation for continued learning.

Care and respect

Supporting a welcoming school community where pupils are treated with dignity and kindness.

Christian values

Building character through faith, service, honesty, and responsibility.
